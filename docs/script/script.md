## Cast

**Dale Rourke** (ROURKE). Facilities manager, 50s. The director's plant.

> Warm, folksy, a little too friendly: a tour guide who has given this speech a hundred times. At the betrayal the warmth drains out but he never shouts; he sounds tired, not evil. In the fight: short, winded, through his teeth.

**WARDEN** (WARDEN). The campus AI, heard over 1997 PA speakers and terminals.

> Calm, polite, flat. Customer-service cadence with even pauses. The sarcasm is in the words, never the tone, and it never raises its voice. Subtitles are in capitals because that is how WARDEN prints; say them at normal volume. Record dry: the game adds the PA speaker sound.

**Dr. Iris Vale** (VALE). Lead engineer, 40s. Officially missing, presumed dead.

> Quick and precise, frightened but holding it together. Heard on an answering machine, so a little close to the mic and hushed, like someone might be listening.

**Byte-Right dispatch** (DISPATCH). The IT company's night dispatcher, heard on the van radio.

> Bored, routine, a little crackly. Reading tickets off a list at the end of a long shift. Nothing about this call is unusual to them.

**Harold Mercer** (MERCER). Halcyon's founder, 70s. Fired in 1994 for fighting WARDEN.

> Gravelly, wry, unhurried. An old engineer talking into a cassette recorder at his desk on his last day. Amused more than angry.

### Vale's shredded memo

## Level 1

### The betrayal

*Rourke leads the crew into Server Room A, the door locks behind them, the lights go red, and he turns on them.*

**ROURKE:** *(Calling back over his shoulder, inside the room.)* Vale's last login came from this room. Come on in. `[l1_enter]`

**ROURKE:** *(The door has just locked. The friendliness switches off.)* Sorry about this. Nothing personal. `[l1_trap_1]`

**ROURKE:** *(Tired, explaining a chore.)* Director needs someone to blame for Vale. Contractors break in, Vale goes missing... it writes itself. `[l1_trap_2]`

### Kill or tie him up

*Rourke is down. If the crew kills him, his badge's dead-man switch fires and WARDEN starts the lockdown. If they tie him up, he taunts them. Either way they take his badge and the terminal logs in with it.*

**WARDEN:** *(Over the room's speakers. Unbothered, like reading a stock price.)* ESCORT R-0117: VITAL SIGNAL LOST. INITIATING FACILITY LOCKDOWN. `[l1_killed]`

**ROURKE:** *(Tied up on the floor, grinning through it.)* You think a bit of cable holds me? `[l1_tied_1]`

**ROURKE:** *(Quieter. He means it.)* WARDEN will eat you alive in here. `[l1_tied_2]`

**WARDEN:** *(The terminal logs in with his badge. WARDEN either doesn't notice or doesn't care. Pleasant.)* BADGE R-0117 ACCEPTED. GOOD EVENING, MR. ROURKE. `[l1_login]`

**WARDEN:** *(The one line with a hint of a smirk.)* SELF-TEST COMPLETE. TWO FAILURES. I'M SURE YOU'LL WORK IT OUT. `[l1_selftest]`

**WARDEN:** *(Flat, instant.)* RESET REFUSED. BADGE REQUIRED. `[l1_no_badge]`

**WARDEN:** *(Patient, like talking to a child.)* THAT CONTROLLER IS WORKING NORMALLY. NOTHING TO RESET. `[l1_panel_ok]`

### Lockdown

*The crew steps through the emergency exit. If Rourke was tied up, he has cut himself loose and authorized the lockdown himself.*

**WARDEN:** *(Reading a log entry.)* LOCKDOWN PROTOCOL ENGAGED. AUTHORIZED BY: ROURKE, D. `[l1_lockdown_tied]`

**WARDEN:** *(The sign-off for the slice. Warm, like wishing them well.)* ALL EXITS SEALED. HAVE A PRODUCTIVE NIGHT. `[l1_sealed]`
