# Rosa Delgado

*Your later-session script for Null & Void: 3 recordings.*

**These lines come later in the story. Please keep them to yourself until you've played that part.**

## Who you are

**Rosa Delgado, 30s.** A school office manager doing nights for extra cash. Organised, unflappable, dry as toast. Treats the crew like a class on a field trip. Twelve years running a school's front office. She treats tonight like any other day of herding people.

**With the crew.** Keeps the notes, counts heads, says "don't touch that" a lot.

**Tonight.** An overnight database job in Halcyon Tower, a 30-storey research tower, starting at eleven. You're learning SQL from a beginner's book as you go. Your lines are you thinking out loud: noticing things, getting stuck, getting it.

**How you sound.** Yourself, as your character: muttering to the crew, not performing. Quick and natural. Never sounds like a hint; it's somebody remembering where they saw something.

**Think of.** A school secretary who has heard every excuse there is.

**Your name.** Players type their own name, so nobody in the game calls you Rosa, and none of your lines says anyone's name.

## How to read this script

- It runs in story order, scene by scene, like a play. Each scene opens with where you are and what's going on.
- Each file name is one recording. Read everything under it in one take, top to bottom.
- What you say is in "quotes". *Italics* say what's happening, what you're doing and how to say it: don't read them out.
- When a recording has more than one line, stop for a full second wherever italics come between them. The game cuts the recording at those pauses.
- A boxed quote is someone else's line, just before yours.

## Recording

- **Format:** WAV, mono, 48 kHz, 16- or 24-bit.
- **Room:** somewhere quiet, with soft things around (a closet or a carpeted room works). Keep the mic a hand's width away.
- **Takes:** two of each recording, in separate files: `<file name>.wav` and `<file name>_take2.wav`.
- **Silence:** half a second at the start and end of every file.
- **Pauses:** in a recording with more than one line, a full second of silence between the lines, longer than any pause inside a line.
- **Effects:** none. Record dry; the game adds speakers, radio static and so on.
- **Level:** the loudest part should sit around -3 dB, never clipping.

Send the files back as they are. They go in `audio/voice/crew_rosa/` in the game.
