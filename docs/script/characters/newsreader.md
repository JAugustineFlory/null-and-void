# Radio newsreader

*Your script for Null & Void: 1 recording.*

## Who you are

A local morning-radio newsreader, reading the third story of the hour.

**Background.** A local morning radio newsreader, years into the job, reading between the traffic and the weather.

**Tonight.** It's the next morning, and the third story of the hour is what happened at Halcyon Tower. This only plays if the crew doesn't make it out.

**How you sound.** Smooth, professional, warm, slightly bored. Calm public-radio delivery with no drama; the flatness is what makes it land.

**Think of.** Public radio at seven in the morning. Don't play the drama.

## How to read this script

- It runs in story order, scene by scene, like a play. Each scene opens with where you are and what's going on.
- Each file name is one recording. Read everything under it in one take, top to bottom.
- What you say is in "quotes". *Italics* say what's happening, what you're doing and how to say it: don't read them out.
- When a recording has more than one line, stop for a full second wherever italics come between them. The game cuts the recording at those pauses.
- A boxed quote is someone else's line, just before yours.

## Recording

- **Format:** WAV, mono, 48 kHz, 16- or 24-bit.
- **Room:** somewhere quiet, with soft things around (a closet or a carpeted room works). Keep the mic a hand's width away.
- **Takes:** two of each recording, in separate files: `<file name>.wav` and `<file name>_take2.wav`.
- **Silence:** half a second at the start and end of every file.
- **Pauses:** in a recording with more than one line, a full second of silence between the lines, longer than any pause inside a line.
- **Effects:** none. Record dry; the game adds speakers, radio static and so on.
- **Level:** the loudest part should sit around -3 dB, never clipping.

Send the files back as they are. They go in `audio/voice/newsreader/` in the game.

## Scene 1: The news (if the crew dies)

**A kitchen, Friday morning, 17 October 1997. A radio is on.**

> You're reading the local morning news on the radio. This is the third story of the hour. In the game it plays over a newspaper lying on a kitchen table. Keep it smooth and even, like any other story.

**[ ] `l2_news_1_block.wav`**

*Only if the whole crew is knocked out on the security floor. The screen goes black, a newspaper fades in on a kitchen table, and the radio begins. At the studio mic, reading from the page. Heard on a kitchen radio. Steady, mid-bulletin.*

"Police are searching for the crew of Byte-Right IT Services, a small firm hired for overnight repairs at Halcyon Data Systems."

*Reading on from the page, same steady pace. No change in tone.*

"Badge records place the crew inside the laboratory of Dr. Iris Vale, who police now believe was murdered."

*Glancing down to find the quote. A slight shift for the quote: reading it, not acting it.*

"A facilities manager who tried to stop them described the crew as, quote, 'nice enough. Nervous. Not who they said they were.'"

*Reading on, one finger on the line. Even, a touch of interest.*

"Byte-Right's chief executive, Maddison Kowalski, was arrested at the Halcyon tower before dawn, trying to force her way inside."

*Reading the quote off the page. Reading the quote flat, not acting it. Then a beat.*

"Ms. Kowalski told officers, quote, 'They were framed! They didn't do it!'"

*Squaring the pages, pausing. Neutral. A small pause after.*

"Halcyon declined to comment. The crew has not been seen since."

*The last line before the weather. Reaching for the weather sheet. Brightening for the weather. The game cuts the audio on 'weather'.*

"Halcyon says the building is safe, and will reopen as normal on Monday. And now, the weather."
