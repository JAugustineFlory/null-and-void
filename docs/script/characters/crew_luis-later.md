# Luis Ortega: later session

You're one of a small IT crew on an overnight job in an office tower, thinking out loud as the crew works things out. Every playable crew member records these same lines in their own voice.

**Who you are:** Luis Ortega, 50s. Handyman, and Maddison's oldest friend. Warm, chatty, and believes everything can be fixed by turning it off and on again. Mostly, he's right. Players type their own name, so nobody in the game calls you Luis.

**How they sound:** Yourself, as your character: muttering to the crew, not performing. Quick and natural. Never sounds like a hint; it's somebody remembering where they saw something.

These lines come later in the story. Please keep them to yourself until after you've played that part.

## Recording

- **Format:** WAV, mono, 48 kHz.
- **Room:** somewhere quiet, with soft things around (a closet or a carpeted room works). Keep the mic a hand's width away.
- **Takes:** two per line, in separate files: `<line_id>.wav` and `<line_id>_take2.wav`.
- **Silence:** half a second at the start and end of every file.
- **Effects:** none. Record dry; the game adds speakers, radio static and so on.
- **Level:** the loudest part should sit around -3 dB, never clipping.

Send the files back as they are. They go in `audio/voice/crew_luis/` in the game.

## Your lines (3)

Each line has the file name to save it as, the line, and a note on how to play it. Don't worry about timing: you'll hear it in the game and we can redo any line after the playtest.
