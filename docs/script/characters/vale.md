# Dr. Iris Vale

*Your script for Null & Void: 3 lines.*

## Who you are

Dr. Iris Vale, the lead engineer, 40s. Calling the night desk late at night about something she'd rather nobody saw.

**Background.** Dr. Iris Vale is Halcyon's lead engineer: brilliant, precise, and the one person who knows how the tower's systems really fit together. Lately she has found something in her work that frightens her, and she has stopped trusting most of the people she works with.

**Tonight.** Late one night, a few days before the crew arrives, she phones the night security desk on the security floor and gets the answering machine. She needs a favour from the night guards, and she can't risk anyone else hearing it.

**The people around you.** The night guards are the people she half trusts. Dale Rourke, from facilities, is someone she warns them about by name.

**What you look like.** Lab coat over a cardigan, reading glasses pushed up into her hair, her badge turned face-in.

**How you sound.** Quick and precise, frightened but holding it together. Heard on an answering machine, so a little close to the mic and hushed, like someone might be listening.

**Think of.** A whistleblower making the call she might regret: someone on a payphone with their back to the street, keeping it short and saying only what has to be said.

## How to read this script

- It runs in story order, scene by scene, like a play. Each scene opens with where you are and what's going on.
- **Cue** is what happens in the game to make you speak. Some lines only play if the players do something, and the cue says so.
- *Just before*, in a conversation, is the line you're answering.
- *(In brackets and italics)* is what you're doing while you speak. Picture it, or do it: it changes how the line comes out.
- Then your line, in bold, and *how to say it*.
- Each line is its own file, with the name above it. Don't worry about timing: you'll hear it in the game, and any line can be redone after the playtest.

## Recording

- **Format:** WAV, mono, 48 kHz.
- **Room:** somewhere quiet, with soft things around (a closet or a carpeted room works). Keep the mic a hand's width away.
- **Takes:** two per line, in separate files: `<line_id>.wav` and `<line_id>_take2.wav`.
- **Silence:** half a second at the start and end of every file.
- **Effects:** none. Record dry; the game adds speakers, radio static and so on.
- **Level:** the loudest part should sit around -3 dB, never clipping.

Send the files back as they are. They go in `audio/voice/vale/` in the game.
