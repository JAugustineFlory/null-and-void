# Ines Oyelaran

*Your script for Null & Void: 1 line.*

## Who you are

Ines Oyelaran, a records clerk on the night shift, 30s. She has been hiding in a locked records cage for an hour because something is sitting at her desk wearing her face.

**Background.** Ines works nights in Personnel's records room on floor 9: filing, copying, keeping the paper side of the company tidy. She likes the night shift because it's quiet.

**Tonight.** An hour ago something sat down at her desk wearing her face. She locked herself in the records cage and has been hiding there ever since, listening. When the crew logs an escort pass with her badge on it, she knows someone real is out there.

**The people around you.** Tomas from payroll is the only other person on her floor tonight. The crew are strangers, and the first people who have come to help.

**What you look like.** Night-shift cardigan, a staff badge on a clip, mascara that has run a little.

**How you sound.** Whispering, fast, trying not to cry and mostly managing. Relief breaks through when the pass comes. Heard from behind a door, then up close.

**Think of.** The survivor in a horror film who has kept it together alone for an hour and is about to find out if it was worth it.

## How to read this script

- It runs in story order, scene by scene, like a play. Each scene opens with where you are and what's going on.
- **Cue** is what happens in the game to make you speak. Some lines only play if the players do something, and the cue says so.
- *Just before*, in a conversation, is the line you're answering.
- *(In brackets and italics)* is what you're doing while you speak. Picture it, or do it: it changes how the line comes out.
- Then your line, in bold, and *how to say it*.
- Each line is its own file, with the name above it. Don't worry about timing: you'll hear it in the game, and any line can be redone after the playtest.

## Recording

- **Format:** WAV, mono, 48 kHz.
- **Room:** somewhere quiet, with soft things around (a closet or a carpeted room works). Keep the mic a hand's width away.
- **Takes:** two per line, in separate files: `<line_id>.wav` and `<line_id>_take2.wav`.
- **Silence:** half a second at the start and end of every file.
- **Effects:** none. Record dry; the game adds speakers, radio static and so on.
- **Level:** the loudest part should sit around -3 dB, never clipping.

Send the files back as they are. They go in `audio/voice/ines/` in the game.
