# Tomas Lindqvist

*Your script for Null & Void: 1 line.*

## Who you are

Tomas Lindqvist, a payroll clerk on the night shift, 50s. He has been under a shelf in a store room since the lights went out, and he's angrier than he is scared.

**Background.** Tomas has done payroll at Halcyon for twenty years. He has seen three reorganisations and a lot of bad coffee, and he is not easily impressed.

**Tonight.** When the lights went out on floor 9, something sat down at his desk. He crawled under a shelf in the payroll store and has been there since, stiff, cold and furious. When the crew logs his escort pass, he finally gets up.

**The people around you.** Ines from records is the only other person on the floor. The crew are strangers, but strangers with a pass, which is more than anyone else has offered.

**What you look like.** Shirt and tie, cardigan, reading glasses on a cord, dust all down one side.

**How you sound.** Low, tired, dry. A man who has decided to be annoyed instead of frightened. Stiff from the floor.

**Think of.** A grumpy middle manager in a disaster film who copes by being annoyed. He is more scared than he will ever admit.

## How to read this script

- It runs in story order, scene by scene, like a play. Each scene opens with where you are and what's going on.
- **Cue** is what happens in the game to make you speak. Some lines only play if the players do something, and the cue says so.
- *Just before*, in a conversation, is the line you're answering.
- *(In brackets and italics)* is what you're doing while you speak. Picture it, or do it: it changes how the line comes out.
- Then your line, in bold, and *how to say it*.
- Each line is its own file, with the name above it. Don't worry about timing: you'll hear it in the game, and any line can be redone after the playtest.

## Recording

- **Format:** WAV, mono, 48 kHz.
- **Room:** somewhere quiet, with soft things around (a closet or a carpeted room works). Keep the mic a hand's width away.
- **Takes:** two per line, in separate files: `<line_id>.wav` and `<line_id>_take2.wav`.
- **Silence:** half a second at the start and end of every file.
- **Effects:** none. Record dry; the game adds speakers, radio static and so on.
- **Level:** the loudest part should sit around -3 dB, never clipping.

Send the files back as they are. They go in `audio/voice/tomas/` in the game.
