# Dale Rourke

*Your script for Null & Void: 43 recordings.*

## Who you are

Dale Rourke, the building's facilities manager, 50s. Tonight he's escorting an IT crew around after hours, and he's friendlier than he needs to be.

**Background.** Halcyon's facilities manager: doors, lights, boilers, badge readers. He's the man everyone calls when something breaks, he knows every shortcut and sticky door, and he tells the same building trivia to every visitor.

**Tonight.** The director has asked him to escort an outside IT crew after hours, and Dale knows what the director really wants from them. On the ground floor and the server floor he's the perfect host. In Secure Lab B, he stops pretending.

**How you sound.** Warm, folksy, a little too friendly: a tour guide who has given this speech a hundred times. At the betrayal the warmth drains out but he never shouts; he sounds tired, not evil. In the fight: short, winded, through his teeth. He laughs once, in the lab: the only time his warmth turns into real glee.

**Think of.** The friendly neighbour in a thriller who turns out to be in on it. The betrayal works because you meant every kind word on the way up.

## How to read this script

- It runs in story order, scene by scene, like a play. Each scene opens with where you are and what's going on.
- Each file name is one recording. Read everything under it in one take, top to bottom.
- What you say is in "quotes". *Italics* say what's happening, what you're doing and how to say it: don't read them out.
- When a recording has more than one line, stop for a full second wherever italics come between them. The game cuts the recording at those pauses.
- A boxed quote is someone else's line, just before yours.

## Recording

- **Format:** WAV, mono, 48 kHz, 16- or 24-bit.
- **Room:** somewhere quiet, with soft things around (a closet or a carpeted room works). Keep the mic a hand's width away.
- **Takes:** two of each recording, in separate files: `<file name>.wav` and `<file name>_take2.wav`.
- **Silence:** half a second at the start and end of every file.
- **Pauses:** in a recording with more than one line, a full second of silence between the lines, longer than any pause inside a line.
- **Effects:** none. Record dry; the game adds speakers, radio static and so on.
- **Level:** the loudest part should sit around -3 dB, never clipping.

Send the files back as they are. They go in `audio/voice/rourke/` in the game.
