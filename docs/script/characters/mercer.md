# Harold Mercer

*Your script for Null & Void: 1 recording.*

## Who you are

Harold Mercer, the company's founder, 70s. Recording a cassette at his desk on his last day, in 1994.

**Background.** He founded Halcyon and ran it for decades. An engineer first, he never trusted computers to run buildings, and when the board wired the campus to WARDEN anyway, they fired him for fighting it.

**Tonight.** It's 1994, his last day. He talks into a cassette recorder for whoever finds the tape one day, then hides it in the building.

**How you sound.** Gravelly, wry, unhurried. An old engineer talking into a cassette recorder at his desk on his last day. Amused more than angry.

**Think of.** An old engineer telling one last story at his own leaving do, enjoying the secret he's keeping.

## How to read this script

- It runs in story order, scene by scene, like a play. Each scene opens with where you are and what's going on.
- Each file name is one recording. Read everything under it in one take, top to bottom.
- What you say is in "quotes". *Italics* say what's happening, what you're doing and how to say it: don't read them out.
- When a recording has more than one line, stop for a full second wherever italics come between them. The game cuts the recording at those pauses.
- A boxed quote is someone else's line, just before yours.

## Recording

- **Format:** WAV, mono, 48 kHz, 16- or 24-bit.
- **Room:** somewhere quiet, with soft things around (a closet or a carpeted room works). Keep the mic a hand's width away.
- **Takes:** two of each recording, in separate files: `<file name>.wav` and `<file name>_take2.wav`.
- **Silence:** half a second at the start and end of every file.
- **Pauses:** in a recording with more than one line, a full second of silence between the lines, longer than any pause inside a line.
- **Effects:** none. Record dry; the game adds speakers, radio static and so on.
- **Level:** the loudest part should sit around -3 dB, never clipping.

Send the files back as they are. They go in `audio/voice/mercer/` in the game.

## Scene 1: Mercer's cassette (optional)

**Harold Mercer's desk, Halcyon Tower. March 1994, his last day. Heard tonight in a forgotten closet behind the lobby's air vent.**

> It's your last day at the company you founded. Tomorrow a machine takes over the building. You sit at your desk with a cassette recorder and talk to whoever finds this tape someday. You want them to understand, and to be ready.

**[ ] `l0_tape_1_block.wav`**

*Only if a player crawls through the lobby's air vent into a forgotten closet and presses play on the cassette on the shelf. Clicking record on the cassette recorder, then leaning in. Dry.*

"Harold Mercer. March of '94. My last day, apparently."

*Sitting back in his chair, recorder in hand. Approving, a little smile.*

"If you're hearing this, you crawled through a vent to get in here. Good. You don't trust doors."

*Gripping the edge of his desk. The one moment of anger, still controlled.*

"Tomorrow a machine runs the locks, the lights and the air. I told the board you don't hand the keys to something that can't be locked out."

*Glancing down at drawings spread across his desk. Quiet, almost proud, then wry on the last sentence.*

"So I've drawn up a room it can't get into. No wires in, no wires out. Whether I ever get to build it is another matter."

*The end of the tape. Leaning close to the recorder, then clicking stop. Slow.*

"If it ever comes to that, look for my plans. The building's records remember more than people do."
