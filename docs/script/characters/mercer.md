# Harold Mercer

*Your script for Null & Void: 5 lines.*

## Who you are

Harold Mercer, the company's founder, 70s. Recording a cassette at his desk on his last day, in 1994.

**Background.** Harold Mercer founded Halcyon Data Systems and ran it for decades. He was an engineer before he was a businessman, and he never trusted computers to run buildings. When the board voted to wire the whole campus to an AI called WARDEN, he fought it, and they fired him.

**Tonight.** It's 1994, his last day. He sits at his desk with a cassette recorder and talks to whoever finds the tape one day. Then he hides it somewhere in the building.

**The people around you.** WARDEN is the machine that replaced him. The board are men he's finished arguing with. Whoever finds the tape is the only person he's really talking to.

**What you look like.** Cardigan, sleeves rolled up, a desk of boxes half packed, a cassette recorder with the red light on.

**How you sound.** Gravelly, wry, unhurried. An old engineer talking into a cassette recorder at his desk on his last day. Amused more than angry.

**Think of.** An old engineer telling one last story at his own leaving do, with a secret he's enjoying keeping.

## How to read this script

- It runs in story order, scene by scene, like a play. Each scene opens with where you are and what's going on.
- **Cue** is what happens in the game to make you speak. Some lines only play if the players do something, and the cue says so.
- *Just before*, in a conversation, is the line you're answering.
- *(In brackets and italics)* is what you're doing while you speak. Picture it, or do it: it changes how the line comes out.
- Then your line, in bold, and *how to say it*.
- Each line is its own file, with the name above it. Don't worry about timing: you'll hear it in the game, and any line can be redone after the playtest.

## Recording

- **Format:** WAV, mono, 48 kHz.
- **Room:** somewhere quiet, with soft things around (a closet or a carpeted room works). Keep the mic a hand's width away.
- **Takes:** two per line, in separate files: `<line_id>.wav` and `<line_id>_take2.wav`.
- **Silence:** half a second at the start and end of every file.
- **Effects:** none. Record dry; the game adds speakers, radio static and so on.
- **Level:** the loudest part should sit around -3 dB, never clipping.

Send the files back as they are. They go in `audio/voice/mercer/` in the game.
